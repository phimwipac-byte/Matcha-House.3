## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a serene, contemporary Japanese tea atelier sensibility, grounded in the principles of *ma* (negative space, pause, and breath) and *wabi-sabi* (unadorned authenticity, quiet warmth, and natural materiality). Designed primarily for mobile web experiences, it serves conscious tea drinkers, aesthetic seekers, and café patrons looking to explore ceremonial matcha grades, reserve tasting sessions, and order handcrafted seasonal drinks.

The interface pairs deliberate restraint with tactile intimacy. Visual density is strictly avoided in favor of quiet, unhurried pacing. Surfaces emulate physical materials—textured washi paper, smooth bamboo utensils, steamed earthenware, and powdered ceremonial green tea. Transitions are soft and decelerated, avoiding mechanical snaps. The visual hierarchy communicates craftsmanship, meditative calm, and effortless modern hospitality.

## Colors

The palette takes direct cues from the Japanese tea ceremony and natural café architecture:

- **Ceremonial Matcha Green (`#2D5A27`)**: The primary brand anchor. Used for high-emphasis actions, key status elements, selected states, and brand focal points. Its deep, rich shade maintains high contrast and visual authority against warm oat grounds.
- **Soft Moss / Sage (`#5B8254`)**: The secondary shade. Evokes tea leaves and bamboo groves; used for secondary badges, progressive indicators, subtle icons, and subdued active states.
- **Golden Bamboo (`#D4A373`)**: The tertiary accent. Reflects the golden hue of split bamboo whisks (*chasen*) and cedar wood finishings. Reserved for artisanal highlights, curated badges, seasonal indicators, and delicate decorative borders.
- **Roasted Charcoal (`#1F2421`)**: The foundational neutral for all typography and structural iconography. Softer and warmer than stark synthetic black, evoking ink stone (*sumi*) calligraphy.
- **Warm Oat Milk & Washed Linen (`#FBF8F2` base, `#F4EFE6` surface)**: Warm, unbleached organic surfaces that replace harsh digital white, providing an inviting, restful canvas.
- **Steamed Ceramic Border (`#E5DCCE`)**: A low-contrast neutral divider tone for cards, dividers, and ghost outlines.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the classical elegance of **EB Garamond** with the gentle, contemporary humanist proportions of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Headline Levels (EB Garamond)**: Conveys literary poise and traditional craftsmanship. Headings should be set with generous line heights and modest letter spacing to give titles room to breathe. Use regular (400) and medium (500) weights to preserve delicate serifs.
- **Body & Interface Levels (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Ensures instant legibility on mobile viewports for menu descriptions, ingredient lists, prep notes, and interactive elements. Its subtle rounded terminals blend harmoniously with the organic visual language.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Rendered with slight tracking expansions (0.04em to 0.08em) in medium weights for subtle, disciplined categorizations (e.g., origin tags, roast levels, ceremonial certifications).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a single-column fluid mobile grid anchored by a minimum 20px (`1.25rem`) lateral safe margin. The philosophy treats whitespace as an active component—allowing each tea item, story section, or preparation step to exist without visual crowding.

- **Mobile Viewports (< 640px)**: Primary focus. 4-column fluid structure with a 16px (`1rem`) gutter and 20px screen margin. Stacked single-card product displays and horizontal card carousels with deliberate bleed to hint at continuation.
- **Tablet & Desktop Viewports (>= 640px, >= 1024px)**: Fluid 8-column and 12-column layouts constrained to a centered maximum content container width of `960px` to maintain intimate editorial proportions rather than stretching into utility dashboard widths.
- **Vertical Cadence**: Vertical spacing scales generously. Section titles sit `3rem` to `4rem` away from neighboring content blocks to evoke the tranquil rhythm of entering a quiet tea garden.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids harsh drop shadows and neon glows in favor of soft, natural ambient shadows and tactile tonal layering. Depth resembles natural morning light passing through a shoji screen onto smooth plaster and glazed ceramic.

- **Base Canvas**: Natural unbleached oat tone (`#FBF8F2`).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards, Menu Rows, Floating Sheets)**: Raised with `#F4EFE6` paired with an ultra-soft, low-opacity warm shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.05)`. A delicate outline (`1px solid #E5DCCE`) defines physical presence.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Active Sheets, Sticky Bottom Actions, Dialogs)**: Clean elevated wash with `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.08)` and subtle backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) over translucent `#FBF8F2E6` surfaces.
- **No Hard Cutoffs**: No harsh borders over 1px thickness; borders mimic the natural texture of handmade paper edges.

## Shapes

The shape language reflects polished river stones, hand-turned chawan (tea bowls), and curved bamboo. Elements use **Level 2 (Rounded)**:

- **Buttons & Chips**: Formed with smooth pill and generous rounded geometries (`rounded-full` for interactive chips and primary action pills; `0.75rem` to `1rem` for cards and input containers).
- **Cards & Modals**: Soft `16px` (`1rem`) corner radii for product cards and article containers; `24px` (`1.5rem`) top radii for mobile bottom sheets and drawers.
- **Imagery Containers**: Rounded corners (`16px` to `20px`) with natural soft clipping that mirrors glazed ceramic tiles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid Ceremonial Matcha Green (`#2D5A27`) background with warm linen typography (`#FBF8F2`). Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), minimum 48px height for effortless mobile thumb touch targets. Subdued hover/tap state shifting to `#23471E`.
- **Secondary Action**: Bordered button with 1px Steamed Ceramic (`#E5DCCE`) border, transparent background, and Roasted Charcoal text (`#1F2421`). Tapping introduces a light `#F4EFE6` fill.
- **Tertiary / Bamboo Accent**: Golden Bamboo background (`#D4A373`) with Charcoal text (`#1F2421`), reserved for special ritual bookings or seasonal additions.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Rounded pill forms (`rounded-full`) with a baseline height of 34px.
- **Default State**: Soft Warm Surface (`#F4EFE6`) with Charcoal text (`#1F2421`) and subtle border (`#E5DCCE`).
- **Selected State**: Ceremonial Matcha Green (`#2D5A27`) background with oat text (`#FBF8F2`).

### Cards & Menu Items
- Built on `#F4EFE6` with `16px` corner rounding and `1px solid #E5DCCE` perimeter borders.
- Inside spacing uses `16px` to `20px` internal padding.
- Product cards prioritize high-resolution imagery of freshly whisked matcha, ceremonial grade provenance stamps, sweet pairing notes, and clear price tags in EB Garamond.

### Input Fields
- Understated container inputs with `#FBF8F2` background, `1px solid #E5DCCE` border, and `12px` rounded corners.
- Active focus transitions the border to Ceremonial Matcha (`#2D5A27`) with a 3px soft tint ring (`rgba(45, 90, 39, 0.12)`). Label typography remains clear and calm in Plus Jakarta Sans.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Radio elements use concentric circles: selected state features a solid Ceremonial Matcha dot within a softened bamboo or sage outer ring.
- Checkboxes use rounded squares (`6px` corner radius) with an organic, hand-drawn check mark in warm linen.

### Specialized Tea Atelier Components
- **Origin Stamp / Grade Tag**: Small, elegant pill tag featuring the Golden Bamboo tone (`#D4A373`) with micro-spaced typography (e.g., "UJI, KYOTO • CEREMONIAL GRADE").
- **Tasting Profile Indicator**: A 5-point horizontal organic dot scale displaying umami, sweetness, bitterness, and body characteristics.
- **Sticky Order Drawer**: A tranquil bottom bar pinned to mobile viewports, finished with washed oat translucent glass (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) and a prominent single-action matcha CTA button.
